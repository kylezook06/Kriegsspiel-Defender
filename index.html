Kriegsspiel Defender – Level 1

Press Space or Enter to start. Move with mouse, WASD, or the arrow keys. Hold Space or Left Click to auto-fire; restart with R after victory or defeat.

The Prussian player block is already wired in from assets/player_block.png. Drop your own PNGs into assets/ (kept gitignored) and extend the preload() block in sketch.js to bring the map and minis to life. Optional audio hooks are ready for BG_Music_Lvl_1.wav and the sound effects listed in the README.
